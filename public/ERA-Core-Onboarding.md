**Version 1.0 · Prepared for prospective ERA Core partners**

## 1. What Is ERA Core?

ERA Core is a **modular, event-driven business operating system** for service-based companies. It provides the foundational platform — data model, scheduling, CRM, pricing, communications, payments, and AI automation — that any field-service business can run on.

ERA Core is **vertical-agnostic by design**. A single business (for example, *VDS Mobile*, a mobile auto-detailing company) is simply one **configuration layer** deployed on top of the same engine. Onboarding a new vertical — HVAC, Roofing, Landscaping, Plumbing, Cleaning — does **not** require rebuilding the platform. It requires configuring it.

This document explains how ERA Core is laid out, and exactly how a new vertical connects to it.

---

## 2. Core Architectural Principles

| Principle | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Event-Driven** | Every meaningful action (booking, completion, payment, review) raises a system event that other modules react to — no monolithic, tightly-coupled logic. |
| **Single Source of Truth** | All business data lives in one database. No sync drift between a CRM, a scheduler, and a billing tool. |
| **Configuration Over Code** | Services, pricing, hours, vehicle/equipment types, membership plans, and feature flags are stored as data — not hardcoded. New verticals configure, they don't redevelop. |
| **Multi-Tenant Ready** | Each business is isolated by a `business_id`. One ERA Core deployment can serve many companies. |
| **Rules-Driven Communications** | All outbound SMS/email pass through a central Communication Rules Engine that enforces consent, business hours, and feature flags before anything is sent. |
| **Audit-First** | Every event and every suppressed communication is recorded immutably for compliance and debugging. |

## 3. The ERA Core Data Model

ERA Core is organized around a central **Job** hub. Everything in the business relates back to a Job.

### 3.1 The Core Entities

```
                     ┌──────────────────┐
                     │  BusinessConfig  │  ← The configuration layer (per vertical)
                     └────────┬─────────┘
                              │ drives
              ┌───────────────┼───────────────┐
              ▼               ▼               ▼
        ┌──────────┐   ┌──────────┐   ┌──────────────┐
        │ Customer │   │   Job    │   │  Contractor  │
        │  (CRM)   │──▶│ (Hub)   │◀──│ (Specialist) │
        └────┬─────┘   └────┬─────┘   └──────────────┘
             │              │
             │              ├──▶ Quote (draft pricing → converts to Job)
             │              ├──▶ Invoice (payment for the Job)
             │              ├──▶ Photos (before/after)
             │              └──▶ SystemEventLog (audit trail)
             │
             ├──▶ CustomerJourney (relationship timeline)
             └──▶ ConversationHistory (AI concierge transcripts)
```

### 3.2 Entity Roles

| Entity | Role in ERA Core |
|---|---|
| **BusinessConfig** | The single source of truth for *how this business operates* — service catalog, pricing rules, hours, scheduling rules, vehicle/equipment classifications, membership plans, feature flags, branding. **This is the vertical's configuration layer.** |
| **Customer** | The CRM entity. Holds profile, contact preferences, consent, lifetime value, and review status. Authentication is separate; this is the business record. |
| **Job** | The operational hub. Every service performed creates a Job. A Job links the customer, the vehicle/equipment/site, the assigned specialist, the schedule, the quote, the invoice, and the photos. |
| **Quote** | A draft pricing configuration generated by the Pricing Engine. Converts into a Job once the customer approves. |
| **Contractor** | The independent specialist/technician profile (detailer, HVAC tech, roofer, etc.). Sets their own availability and service areas; no clock-in/timesheets. |
| **Invoice** | Payment record for a completed Job. Supports cash, card, check, and Stripe. |
| **CustomerJourney** | Auto-generated, immutable timeline of every customer interaction — tells the story of the relationship. |
| **SystemEventLog** | Immutable audit trail of every platform event and every suppressed communication. |
| **ConversationHistory** | Transcript history for the AI concierge, keyed by customer phone. |

> **Vertical-specific entities** (e.g., VDS Mobile's `MemberVehicle` and `VehicleSubscription`) sit *alongside* the core. They are owned by the vertical's configuration, not by ERA Core itself.

## 4. The Engines

ERA Core ships with a set of reusable engines that power every vertical.

### 4.1 Pricing Engine
- Reads **entirely** from BusinessConfig — no hardcoded prices.
- Computes quotes from: service selected × vehicle/equipment classification × condition multiplier × add-ons.
- Condition can affect both **price** (multiplier) and **duration** (added minutes).
- Always computed server-side; client-supplied prices are discarded to prevent fraud.

### 4.2 Scheduling Engine
- Checks availability against business hours, booking buffers, minimum notice, and max bookings per day.
- Auto-assigns the best-fit specialist based on skill, weekly availability, blocked dates, service area, and current workload.
- Mirrors bookings to a shared calendar (Google Calendar) as a live event, while Base44 remains the source of truth.
- Prevents double-booking at both the calendar and specialist level.

### 4.3 Communication Rules Engine
Every outbound SMS/email is evaluated *before* delivery:
- Is the customer's SMS/email consent flag set?
- Is the global SMS feature flag enabled (e.g., until A2P carrier registration is approved)?
- Is this a duplicate (idempotency flags prevent re-sending)?
- Is the customer opted out of promotions or reviews?

If any rule blocks the message, it is **suppressed and logged** (with the reason) to the SystemEventLog — never silently dropped. When SMS is suppressed, reminders fall back to email automatically.

### 4.4 AI Concierge (Valerie)
- SMS-based AI assistant that answers questions, generates quotes, books appointments, and checks membership status.
- Grounded in the live BusinessConfig catalog and the customer's own history.
- Escalates complex or sensitive requests to a human specialist.
- All actions are logged to the SystemEventLog and ConversationHistory.

### 4.5 Payments & Memberships
- One-time services billed offline (cash/card/check) and tracked via Invoices.
- Recurring memberships (e.g., VDS Gold) billed through Stripe subscriptions.
- Webhooks provision membership access only after validating against BusinessConfig — preventing unauthorized premium features.

## 5. How VDS Mobile Connects to ERA Core

VDS Mobile is the **first vertical** deployed on ERA Core. It demonstrates how a specific business maps onto the generic engine.

| ERA Core (generic) | VDS Mobile (configured) |
|---|---|
| BusinessConfig | "VDS Mobile" — detailing services, 4 vehicle classifications, 2 pricing groups, VDS Gold membership |
| Customer | A car owner |
| Job | A detailing appointment (Full Detail, Exterior Detail, etc.) |
| Quote | A custom detailing quote (vehicle + condition + services → price) |
| Contractor | A mobile detail specialist |
| Vehicle classification | Coupe, Sedan, Mid-Size SUV, Truck / 3-Row SUV |
| Condition multiplier | Light / Moderate / Heavy wear (affects price + duration) |
| Membership | VDS Gold (recurring monthly, per-vehicle, via Stripe) |
| AI Concierge | "Valerie" — SMS concierge for quotes & booking |
| Vertical entities | `MemberVehicle`, `VehicleSubscription`, `ServiceRecord` |

**The key insight:** VDS Mobile adds *no new engine logic*. It configures BusinessConfig, registers its services and pricing, defines its vehicle classifications, and connects its Stripe products. Everything else — scheduling, CRM, pricing, communications, audit — is inherited from ERA Core.

## 6. Onboarding a New Vertical (HVAC or Roofing Example)

This is the exact path a new partner takes. Notice that **no engine code changes** are required.

### Step 1 — Configure BusinessConfig
Define the new vertical's identity and rules:

```jsonc
{
  "business_id": "acme-hvac",
  "business_name": "Acme HVAC",
  "timezone": "America/New_York",
  "business_hours": [ /* Mon–Fri 8–18, Sat 9–14, Sun closed */ ],
  "scheduling_rules": {
    "booking_buffer_hours": 2,
    "min_notice_hours": 24,
    "slot_interval_minutes": 120,
    "max_bookings_per_day": 6
  }
}
```

### Step 2 — Define the Service Catalog
Add the services this vertical performs, each with per-tier pricing and duration:

| VDS Mobile | → | HVAC | → | Roofing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full Detail | | AC Tune-Up | | Roof Inspection |
| Exterior Detail | | System Diagnostics | | Shingle Repair |
| Ceramic Coating | | Full System Install | | Full Roof Replacement |

Each service stores tiers (price + duration) keyed by the relevant classification (vehicle type / system type / roof type).

### Step 3 — Define Classifications & Pricing Groups
- **HVAC:** `residential_split`, `commercial_rooftop`, `mini_split`, `heat_pump`
- **Roofing:** `asphalt_shingle`, `metal`, `tile`, `flat_commercial`

Map each classification to a pricing group, just as VDS maps `coupe/sedan → sedan_coupe` and `truck/suv → truck_suv`.

### Step 4 — Define Condition / Complexity Multipliers
VDS uses Light / Moderate / Heavy *wear*. A vertical can rename this concept:
- **HVAC:** `routine` / `dirty_filters` / `neglected_system` (affects price + labor time)
- **Roofing:** `minor` / `moderate` / `severe` damage (same mechanic)

Each multiplier carries a price multiplier **and** a `duration_add_minutes` so estimates scale realistically.

### Step 5 — Register Specialists (Contractors)
Create Contractor profiles for each technician. They set their own weekly availability, blocked dates, skills (e.g., `install`, `repair`, `maintenance`), and service areas (counties + max travel distance). The scheduling engine handles the rest.

### Step 6 — Connect Payments (if applicable)
- One-time work → tracked via Invoices (cash/card/check).
- Recurring service plans (e.g., an HVAC "Comfort Club" maintenance membership) → Stripe subscription products, referenced from BusinessConfig.

### Step 7 — Brand the AI Concierge
Give the concierge a name and persona for the vertical (VDS calls theirs "Valerie"). The system prompt is built from BusinessConfig — the catalog, hours, and rules — so the same engine speaks the new vertical's language automatically.

### Step 8 — Go Live
Once BusinessConfig is populated and specialists are registered, the vertical inherits:
- A customer-facing pricing & booking page
- A member/customer portal
- A specialist portal (job board, availability, completion flow)
- An admin dashboard (metrics, jobs, quotes, invoices, communications, customer journeys)
- Automated reminders, review requests, and AI concierge — all rules-driven

## 7. What Changes vs. What Stays

| | Stays the Same (ERA Core) | Changes Per Vertical (BusinessConfig) |
|---|---|---|
| **Data model** | Customer, Job, Quote, Invoice, Contractor, CustomerJourney, SystemEventLog | Classifications, pricing groups, services, condition labels |
| **Engines** | Pricing, Scheduling, Communication Rules, AI Concierge, Payments | Service catalog, hours, buffers, skill names |
| **Portals** | Admin, Specialist, Member/Customer layouts | Branding, labels, vertical-specific entities |
| **Communications** | Consent enforcement, suppression logging, email fallback | Message copy, concierge persona, review link |

**The ratio is roughly 90% inherited / 10% configured.** This is what makes onboarding a new vertical a matter of days, not months.

## 8. Compliance, Audit & Trust

- **Consent-first:** No SMS or email is sent without an explicit consent flag on the customer record.
- **Immutable audit:** Every event and every *suppressed* message is written to the SystemEventLog with a reason.
- **Customer journey:** Administrators get a full, human-readable relationship timeline per customer.
- **Server-side pricing:** Quotes are always recomputed server-side from BusinessConfig — customers cannot forge a price.
- **Ownership-verified actions:** Appointment changes and cancellations require verified identity — never a guessable email or phone alone.
